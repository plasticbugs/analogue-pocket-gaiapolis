**World map colours and the music, after the MiSTer port.** Two fixes from
decoryah's [Arcade-Gaiapolis_MiSTer](https://github.com/decoryah/Arcade-Gaiapolis_MiSTer),
which measured this core's RTL against a real board and against MAME's audio.

### What's fixed

- **The dark squares on the world map are gone.** The rotating background's
  palette numbers come two tiles to a byte, and MAME (and this core after
  it) read the two halves the wrong way round. It only showed where the
  colour changes between the two tiles of a byte: the edge of every place
  name and route line on the world map, dark where a route is still unlit,
  and a few off-colour edge tiles in the intro's flight over the land. A
  recording of a real board has neither. (MAME shows the squares too: MAME
  Testers 00231, reported in 2008.)
- **The K054539 follows MAME's chip more closely**: 16-bit samples step two
  bytes from whatever address the channel has, a write to a channel's
  position registers waits for that channel's key-on (so programming the
  next note while the last still sounds no longer restarts it with a burst
  of noise), the reverb ring index adds the position twice, the two chips'
  sum saturates instead of wrapping, and the left and right channels follow
  MAME's routing, which is the opposite of the earlier builds. The MiSTer
  port measured the per-second correlation with MAME's own audio of the
  game going from 0.915 to 0.964 with these.

### What was checked

- The frozen-state ROZ bench (24 MAME states, the RTL against the reference
  renderer pixel for pixel) passes with the new order; the reference
  renderer still matches MAME's snapshots pixel for pixel when asked to
  read the nibbles MAME's way.
- The RTL lints clean; the whole machine boots through its self-test in
  simulation with the new sound chip.
- **Not yet tried on a Pocket.** This bitstream is CI's compile of the
  tagged commit, not a board-tested one: the video path is untouched, the
  changes are one line in the rotating plane and the sound chip's
  internals. If the music or stereo sounds wrong to you, say so.

### Installing

Unzip onto the SD card root, overwriting previous files. ROMs are not
included: build them with the included `mra_build.py` from your own MAME
romset, as the README describes. Your `gaiapols.sav` carries over.
